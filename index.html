01 — OUR PURPOSE

Helping cats.
Helping our community.

Purrpose Paws is a project created by Koharu Hato and Cara Zeng at Park Tudor School.

We hope to work with a local animal welfare organization to identify a real need and create a meaningful community giving project around it.

02 — WHAT WE DO
