🐾 Purrpose Paws

Community Giving Project 2026–2027

A student-led community giving project supporting cats in need through fundraising, donations, and community outreach.

Our Project

Purrpose Paws is a project created by Koharu Hato and Cara Zeng at Park Tudor School. We hope to partner with Indy Humane to identify a real need and create a meaningful community giving project around it.

Our Goals

  • Support cats in need
  • Engage our local community
  • Raise funds or collect needed supplies
  • Volunteer and give back
  • Measure our impact
